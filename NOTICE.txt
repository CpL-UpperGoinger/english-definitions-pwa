Dictionary content
English definitions are derived from English Wiktionary and obtained in
structured form through FreeDictionaryAPI.com.

Wiktionary: https://en.wiktionary.org/
FreeDictionaryAPI.com: https://freedictionaryapi.com/

Wiktionary text is also available under the GNU Free Documentation License.
Entries were selected, normalized, and reformatted for local search; definition
text was not edited. Each entry links back to its original Wiktionary page.
No dictionary audio is bundled. No account, advertising, analytics, or tracking
service is included.

The bundled data set is a curated starter collection. Its exact entry count,
build date, source information, missing entries, and fetch failures are recorded
in data/meta.json and data/build-report.json. To expand or refresh it, edit
tools/words.txt and run tools/build_dictionary.py locally.
