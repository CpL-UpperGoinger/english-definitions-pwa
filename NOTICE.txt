Dictionary content
Bundled English definitions are derived from English Wiktionary and obtained in
structured form through kaikki.org / Wiktextract. Optional user-initiated online
lookups use FreeDictionaryAPI.com and are cached only in the current browser.

Wiktionary: https://en.wiktionary.org/
kaikki.org: https://kaikki.org/dictionary/English/index.html
FreeDictionaryAPI.com: https://freedictionaryapi.com/

https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Wiktionary text is also available under the GNU Free Documentation License.
Entries were selected, normalized, and reformatted for local search; definition
text was not edited. Each entry links back to its original Wiktionary page.
No dictionary audio is bundled. No account, advertising, analytics, or tracking
service is included.

The bundled data set is a frequency-ranked, curated collection of approximately
100,000 entries. Its exact entry count, build date, source information and build
statistics are recorded in data/meta.json and data/build-report.json. Rebuild it
with tools/build_large_dictionary.py; the multi-gigabyte source is not deployed.
The smaller API-based builder remains available as tools/build_dictionary.py.
